Cooperation beyond a fixed role

Agents must discover a target, request and accept a handoff, then confirm continued tracking. Roles emerge from observations and task state. The current SubT study varies target appearance, agent identity and teammate motion.

What qualifies for the leaderboard?

Rankings use completed model-controlled PX4/Gazebo episodes with public inputs, independent audits and verified action traces. Teacher demonstrations, camera proxies and offline replay remain diagnostic evidence.

03 /From observations to evidence

A consistent evaluation contract for models, tasks and scenes.

01

Observe & cooperate

Models receive public observations and delivered messages. Target truth and private evaluator state stay outside policy inputs.

02

Execute & verify

Log model responses, executed actions and outcomes. Every new run requires independent audit before it becomes eligible evidence.

03

Evaluate transfer

Keep training separate from held-out routes, scenes and tasks. Aggregate eligible completed episodes; report exclusions and coverage alongside scores.
